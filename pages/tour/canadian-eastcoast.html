East Coast of Canada

5 Nights 6 Days

Overview

The trip opens in Toronto. You make your own way to the hotel and check in, and the rest of that first day is yours. It is a good afternoon to walk the downtown streets, look up at the towers and eat somewhere you picked yourself. Nothing is scheduled until the next morning, so you can sleep off the flight or stay out late. From Toronto the route runs east over the following days: Niagara Falls, the 1000 Islands, Ottawa, Montreal and a full day in Quebec City.

What's Included?

  • Sightseeing, excursions, meals and hotels as set out in the itinerary on this page.
    • Meals:
  • Lunch and dinner come as a set spread: two vegetarian dishes, one non-vegetarian dish, lentils, rice, naan, salad, yoghurt, sides and a dessert.
    • Medical Insurance:
  • Standard cover for the length of the tour, COVID-19 included, is in the tour price. Travellers over 60, and anyone extending their stay, pay an additional charge.
    • Internal Tickets
  • The internal tickets you need to take part in the tour are covered, priced at the rates in force on 1 September 2023.
    • Baggage
  • The operating airline's handling fee for one checked bag per sector.

What's Excluded?

  • Tips for local guides, drivers and hotel staff: CAD 5 per person per day for the whole tour, handed to the tour manager on day one.
  • Flying out on a date other than the group's departure from India, returning on a day other than the end of the tour, or using a different start or end city. Any of these costs extra, depends on seats being free on the dates you want and on ticket validity, and your sales contact or travel agent will quote the difference. Deviations are only actioned 30 days before the tour departs.
  • Taxes, which are charged on top.
  • Handling fees for extra checked bags, and for any additional internal flight sectors.
  • Passport fees, port-of-entry charges and the like.
  • The Basic Travel Quota, equivalent to USD 2,50,000.
  • Any airfare increase the airline applies to your ticket. Fares here are those in force on 1 September 2023.

Itinerary

Day 1

Hotel Monte Carlo Inn or Similar in Toronto

Breakfast and Dinner

Arrive in Toronto and make your own way to the hotel to check in. The rest of the day is free.

Day 2

Hotel Sheraton on the falls or similar in niagara

Breakfast and Dinner

Toronto city tour, the CN Tower observation deck, Niagara Falls, the Hornblower cruise and the Skylon Tower.

The guided tour of Toronto comes first, passing City Hall, Queen's Park, Chinatown and the University of Toronto. Then up the CN Tower to the observation deck; at 553 metres it held the record for the world's tallest free-standing structure for over thirty years. After that we drive to Niagara Falls and board the Hornblower, which takes you close enough to the base of the falls to get wet. (Weather decides this one: the Hornblower runs from the last week of May.) The day ends at the Skylon Tower observation deck, which looks out over the American and Canadian sides at once.

Day 3

Hotel Four Points by Sheraton Gatineau or Similar in Ottawa

Breakfast and dinner

Cruise through the 1000 Islands, then drive on to Ottawa.

We drive to Kingston and board a boat through the 1000 Islands, an archipelago strung along the St. Lawrence River with the Canada-US border running between the islands. Some carry castles, some hold a single house and a tree. Afterwards we continue to Ottawa, Canada's capital.

Day 4

Hotel Courtyard Marriott Montreal Airport or similar in Montreal

Breakfast and dinner

Orientation tour of Ottawa, then a guided tour of Montreal.

An orientation drive around Ottawa takes in Parliament Hill, the Prime Minister's residence and the Rideau Canal, which freezes into a skating rink every winter. We then drive to Montreal for a guided city tour: Mount Royal Park, and a loop past the Old Port, Saint Joseph's Oratory and Notre-Dame Basilica.

Day 5

Hotel Courtyard Marriott Montreal Airport or similar in Montreal

Breakfast and dinner

Guided tour of Quebec City and a stop at Montmorency Falls.

A full day out to Quebec City. The guided tour runs through the Upper and Lower Towns and past the Parliament Buildings, the Plains of Abraham, the Citadel, the Chateau Frontenac, Place d'Armes and the National Assembly. On the way back we stop at Montmorency Falls, which drops 83 metres, roughly 30 metres further than Niagara.

Day 6

Breakfast

Heading home.

Make your own way to the airport; the tour ends here. If you have a few minutes, write and tell us how it went. It helps the group that comes after you.
