East Coast of Canada

5 Nights 6 Days

Overview

The trip opens in Toronto. You make your own way to the hotel and check in, and the rest of that first day is yours. It is a good afternoon to walk the downtown streets, look up at the towers and eat somewhere you picked yourself. Nothing is scheduled until the next morning, so you can sleep off the flight or stay out late. From Toronto the route runs east over the following days: Niagara Falls, the 1000 Islands, Ottawa, Montreal and a full day in Quebec City.

What's Included?

  • Sightseeing, excursions, meals and hotels as set out in the itinerary on this page.
    • Meals:
  • Lunch and dinner come as a set spread: two vegetarian dishes, one non-vegetarian dish, lentils, rice, naan, salad, yoghurt, sides and a dessert.
    • Medical Insurance:
  • Standard cover for the length of the tour, COVID-19 included, is in the tour price. Travellers over 60, and anyone extending their stay, pay an additional charge.
    • Internal Tickets
  • The internal tickets you need to take part in the tour are covered, priced at the rates in force on 1 September 2023.
    • Baggage
  • The operating airline's handling fee for one checked bag per sector.

What's Excluded?

  • Gratuities for local guides, coach drivers and hotel staff, set at CAD 5 per person for each day of the trip and given to the tour manager at the start.
  • Choosing your own flight dates or a different departure or return city instead of the group's schedule from India. Such changes carry an added cost, hinge on seat availability and ticket validity, and your agent can price the difference. They can only be processed once the tour is within 30 days of departure.
  • Taxes, which are charged on top.
  • Handling fees for extra checked bags, and for any additional internal flight sectors.
  • Passport fees, port-of-entry charges and the like.
  • The Basic Travel Quota allowance (equal to USD 2,50,000).
  • Any airfare increase the airline applies to your ticket. Fares here are those in force on 1 September 2023.

Itinerary

Day 1

Overnight in Toronto: Monte Carlo Inn, or a comparable hotel

Breakfast and Dinner

Land in Toronto and travel to the hotel under your own steam. Once checked in, you have the day to yourself.

Day 2

Overnight at Niagara: the Sheraton on the Falls, or a comparable hotel

Breakfast and Dinner

Toronto city tour, the CN Tower observation deck, Niagara Falls, the Hornblower cruise and the Skylon Tower.

A guide leads the morning around Toronto, with stops by City Hall, Queen's Park, Chinatown and the university campus. You then ride up the CN Tower to the viewing deck. At 553 metres, it stood as the tallest free-standing structure on earth for more than thirty years. From there the road leads to Niagara Falls, where the Hornblower boat carries you right up to the foot of the cascades and you will be splashed. The boats only sail from the final week of May, so the season matters. You finish at the Skylon Tower deck, with the American and Canadian falls spread out side by side.

Day 3

Overnight in Ottawa: Four Points by Sheraton Gatineau, or a comparable hotel

Breakfast and dinner

Cruise through the 1000 Islands, then drive on to Ottawa.

We drive to Kingston and board a boat through the 1000 Islands, an archipelago strung along the St. Lawrence River with the Canada-US border running between the islands. Some carry castles, some hold a single house and a tree. Afterwards we continue to Ottawa, Canada's capital.

Day 4

Overnight in Montreal: Courtyard Marriott near the airport, or a comparable hotel

Breakfast and dinner

Orientation tour of Ottawa, then a guided tour of Montreal.

Start with a drive-through of Ottawa that passes Parliament Hill, the Prime Minister's residence and the Rideau Canal, which turns into a long skating rink each winter. After that we head to Montreal, where a local guide shows you Mount Royal Park, the Old Port, Saint Joseph's Oratory and Notre-Dame Basilica.

Day 5

Overnight in Montreal: Courtyard Marriott near the airport, or a comparable hotel

Breakfast and dinner

Guided tour of Quebec City and a stop at Montmorency Falls.

Today is a long excursion to Quebec City. Your guide takes you around the old Upper and Lower Towns, the Plains of Abraham, the Citadel, Chateau Frontenac, Place d'Armes and the Parliament and National Assembly buildings. On the return leg we pause at Montmorency Falls, which plunges 83 metres, about 30 more than Niagara.

Day 6

Breakfast

Heading home.

Make your own way to the airport; the tour ends here. If you have a few minutes, write and tell us how it went. It helps the group that comes after you.
