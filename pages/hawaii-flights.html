        Why People Fly to Hawaii

        Out in the central Pacific, roughly five and a half hours by plane from the West Coast, Hawaii is a string of volcanic islands. Six welcome travellers, and each has its own personality. Oahu brings the big city, the history and the greatest number of flights. Maui is known for its resorts and the winding road to Hana. Kauai is all sea cliffs, while the Big Island offers an erupting volcano and the telescopes of Mauna Kea. Across the group you will find black-sand beaches, rainforest, high desert and real cattle ranches. Hawaiian language, hula and cooking are living traditions here rather than a show put on for visitors, and more people arrive every year, many of them returning to a favourite island.

        Getting to Hawaii

        • By Air

          The busiest gateway is Honolulu, where Daniel K. Inouye International (HNL) receives the bulk of arrivals. Maui uses Kahului (OGG), the Big Island has Kona (KOA) and Hilo (ITO), and Kauai has Lihue (LIH). A number of these accept nonstop mainland flights too, so a stop in Honolulu is not always necessary.

        • By Cruise

          Cruise itineraries loop around the islands, pausing at Oahu and Maui, then Kauai, then the Big Island, usually for a day apiece. Sailing lets you visit four islands while unpacking only once, since your cabin travels with you.

        • By Road

          No highway leads to Hawaii, and most islands have no passenger ferry between them, so moving from one to another means a flight. After you land, a rental car is the easiest way to explore at your own pace, and on Maui and Kauai the scenic coastal drive is a highlight in itself.

        Hawaii's Airports

        Each inhabited island has at least one airport, and a flight between neighbouring islands lasts about thirty minutes. Honolulu takes the long-distance arrivals and most of the transfers. Kahului, Kona and Lihue likewise accept direct flights from the mainland, while the others are small airstrips used for brief local hops. Here is the list to choose from:

        • Kahului, Maui (OGG)

        • Kona, Big Island: Ellison Onizuka International (KOA)

        • Honolulu, Oahu: Daniel K. Inouye International (HNL)

        • Lihue, Kauai (LIH)

        • Hilo, Big Island (ITO)

        • Hana, Maui (HNM)

        • Molokai (MKK)

        • Waimea-Kohala, Big Island (MUE)

        • Lanai (LNY)

        • Kalaupapa, Molokai (LUP)
