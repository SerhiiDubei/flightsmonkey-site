        Why People Fly to Alaska

        Alaska sits in the far northwest of North America, and the scale of it is the first thing people notice. There are roughly 100,000 glaciers, mountain ranges you can watch from the plane for an hour, and thousands of miles of coast. Brown bears, moose and humpback whales are ordinary here rather than an event. Alaska Native peoples have lived across the state for thousands of years, and their languages, carving and weaving are still part of daily life. In winter the aurora shows up over the interior on clear nights; in summer the sun barely leaves. The cooking follows what comes out of the water: salmon, halibut, king crab. Visitor numbers have climbed steadily for years.

        Getting to Alaska

        • By Air

          Almost every long-haul flight lands at Ted Stevens Anchorage International Airport (ANC).

        • By Cruise

          Ships work the Inside Passage and the Gulf of Alaska, calling at Whittier, Juneau and Seward.

        • By Road

          The Alaska Highway climbs up through Canada into the state. It is a long drive with mountains most of the way.

        • By Rail

          The Alaska Railroad links Anchorage, Denali and Fairbanks, with small stops in between.

        Alaska's Airports

        Roads reach only a small part of Alaska, so the airports do the work. Anchorage takes most of the arrivals from outside the state and feeds the rest of the network. Smaller fields like Bethel, Nome and Utqiagvik serve towns with no road in at all, which is why a short flight can be the only way there. These are the ones you are most likely to pass through:

        • Ted Stevens Anchorage International Airport (ANC)

        • Fairbanks International Airport (FAI)

        • Juneau International Airport (JNU)

        • Kotzebue Ralph Wien Memorial Airport (OTZ)

        • Bethel Airport (BET)

        • Nome Airport (OME)

        • Dillingham Airport (DLG)

        • Kodiak Benny Benson State Airport (ADQ)

        • Ketchikan International Airport (KTN)

        • Sitka Rocky Gutierrez Airport (SIT)

        • Barrow Wiley Post-Will Rogers Memorial Airport (BRW)
