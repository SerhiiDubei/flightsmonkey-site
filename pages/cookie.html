Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy describes the way cookies are used on the {{SITE_NAME}} website, which you can reach at www.{{SITE_DOMAIN}}.

COOKIES IN BRIEF

A cookie is a small file saved on your device to make browsing smoother. Below you will find what cookies collect, how that information is put to work and the reasons we may need to store them. We also point out how to stop them from being saved, though doing so can break parts of the site.

If you would like a broader explanation of how cookies work, the Wikipedia entry on HTTP Cookies is a good place to start.

OUR REASONS FOR USING COOKIES

We rely on cookies for the purposes listed further down. In most cases there is no standard way to switch them off without affecting how the site behaves. If you are not sure whether you need a particular cookie, the safest choice is to leave all of them on, since they may support a service you are using.

TURNING COOKIES OFF

Your browser settings let you block cookies from being saved; check your browser's Help section for the steps. Be aware that blocking them can change how this site, and plenty of others, work. We suggest leaving cookies enabled.

COOKIES FROM US

Account Cookies:

  • Support the sign-up process and everyday account administration after you register.
  • They are normally removed when you sign out.

Sign-in Cookies:

  • Keep you recognised as signed in, so you are not asked to log in again on every page.
  • They are usually cleared when you sign out.

Newsletter Cookies:

  • Store your choices about newsletters and email subscriptions.
  • May decide whether certain notices are displayed to people who have or have not subscribed.

Order Cookies:

  • Needed so that orders and payments are handled correctly during a purchase.

Survey Cookies:

  • Used in surveys and questionnaires to note whether you have taken part and to keep the results accurate.

Form Cookies:

  • Created when you send information through a form, such as a contact or comment form, so your details can be remembered for later replies.

Preference Cookies:

  • Record the way you like the site to behave, so the experience can be tailored to your choices.

COOKIES FROM OTHER PROVIDERS

At times we also depend on cookies supplied by reputable outside companies. These are the ones you could come across:

Google Analytics:

  • A popular analytics tool that shows us how the site is used so we can make it better for visitors.
  • Its cookies log details such as how long you stay and which pages you open.

Other Analytics Tools:

  • Used to gauge how the site is used, so we can improve our content.

Testing and Optimization Cookies:

  • Applied while we trial new features, keeping your experience steady and revealing what visitors prefer.

Purchase Tracking Cookies:

  • Tell us how many visitors end up buying, which helps with business forecasting.

Affiliate Cookies:

  • Let us credit partner sites that send customers our way, so those affiliates can reward purchases.

Social Media Cookies:

  • Placed by social sharing buttons and plugins; they may enrich your social network profile or add to the information those networks already hold.

PROTECTING YOUR INFORMATION

Whatever personal details you give us are kept secure and used only for the reasons given in this policy.
