Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies on the {{SITE_NAME}} website, accessible from www.{{SITE_DOMAIN}}.

WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are tiny files that are downloaded to your computer, enhancing your online experience. This policy outlines the information cookies gather, how we use it, and why storing these cookies may be necessary. We also provide guidance on preventing cookie storage, although this may impact certain elements of site functionality.

For more general information on cookies, you can refer to the Wikipedia article on HTTP Cookies.

HOW WE USE COOKIES

We use cookies for various reasons, as detailed below. Unfortunately, in most cases, there are no standard options for disabling cookies without impacting site functionality. It is recommended to leave all cookies enabled if you are unsure whether you need them, as they may be used to provide services you use.

DISABLING COOKIES

You can prevent cookies from being set by adjusting your browser settings (see your browser Help for instructions). However, disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this and many other websites. It is recommended not to disable cookies.

THE COOKIES WE SET

Account-related Cookies:

  • Used for the management of the signup process and general administration when you create an account.
  • These cookies are usually deleted upon logging out.

Login-related Cookies:

  • Used to remember your login status, eliminating the need to log in on each page visit.
  • These cookies are typically cleared upon logging out.

Email Newsletters-related Cookies:

  • Utilized to remember your newsletter or email subscription preferences.
  • May determine whether to show certain notifications to subscribed/unsubscribed users.

Orders Processing-related Cookies:

  • Essential for ensuring proper order processing during e-commerce or payment transactions.

Surveys-related Cookies:

  • Used for user surveys and questionnaires, remembering participation status or providing accurate results.

Forms-related Cookies:

  • Set when you submit data through forms, such as contact pages or comment forms, to remember user details for future correspondence.

Site Preferences Cookies:

  • Used to set preferences for how the site runs, providing a customized experience based on user preferences.

THIRD-PARTY COOKIES

In some cases, we use cookies provided by trusted third parties. Here are details on third-party cookies you might encounter:

Google Analytics:

  • Widely used analytics solution to understand site usage and improve user experience.
  • These cookies track information like time spent on the site and pages visited.

Third-party Analytics:

  • Employed to measure site usage for content improvement.

Testing and Optimization Cookies:

  • Used during feature testing to ensure a consistent user experience and understand user preferences.

Purchase Tracking Cookies:

  • Essential for understanding how many site visitors make purchases, aiding in business predictions.

Affiliate Tracking Cookies:

  • Allow us to credit partner sites for customer referrals and enable affiliates to provide bonuses for purchases.

Social Media Cookies:

  • Set by social media buttons/plugins, enhancing your social network profile or contributing to the data held by social media sites.

INFORMATION SECURITY

We assure you that the personal details you share with us will be safe and secure. They will only be used for the purposes outlined in this policy.
